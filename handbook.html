Items

## Getting items Items are obtained through **Summoning**. Once summoned, items go to your **Inventory**. ## Viewing items On the **Stats** menu, you can click on your Inventory page to view items stored there. You can then open the **Item Inspector** to use an item: upon opening, the Inspector lets you select an item by *index* or *equipment type*, depending on whether it is in your inventory or in your equipment. An item’s *inventory index* (or “index” or “number”) is the upper number on its frame shown on your Inventory page. ## Using items From an item’s page on the Inspector, you can: * **equip** it, replacing any previous equipment for the item’s *type* (Weapon, Armor, Necklace and so on); * **upgrade** it, using other items as materials selected on the dropdown menu opened upon selecting this option; * **reroll** its Secret Substat, if the item holds it. ## Upgrading items Item upgrades are based on how many materials you use, and on their rarity: * 2 ★ items grant **10 points**; * 3 ★ items grant **20 points**; * 4 ★ items grant **40 points**; * 5 ★ items grant **80 points**.
Each *rarity* (or “star level”) has a maximum upgrade capacity: * 2 ★ items can reach up to **level 5**; * 3 ★ items can reach up to **level 10**; * 4 ★ items can reach up to **level 15**; * 5 ★ items can reach up to **level 20**. ## Limit break Items’ **star level** can be raised via *limit breaking* (or “star level increase”, or “rarity increase”): this happens automatically when upgrading an item, under the following conditions: * said material must be **another copy** of the item to be upgraded (same name); * said material must be **equal** or **higher** in **star level**; however, * said item’s **level does not influence these conditions**.
When a limit break is performed, another bonus is granted: the item receives a **Secret Substat**, that: * is a **new stat**, meaning it is not present in the original copy of the selected item; * is assigned upon the **first limit break** of said item; * is then **increased** on its next limit breaks. ## Rerolling a Secret Substat For **4 ★ or lower** items, it is possible to reroll their Secret Substat, using a material that follows the **same conditions as limit break materials**. ## LUCK **LUCK** is a stat that is currently only available as **Secret Substat**, meaning it is only obtainable through limit breaking or rerolling, and it positively influences various aspects of the game as explained in the **Stats guide** on the Stats menu.
