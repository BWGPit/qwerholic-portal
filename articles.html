To make a new expansion

After more than one year since the first card set, QWERHolic cards make a comeback with the second expansion

While the initial idea was to make a smaller set, there were certainly enough material to build up a whole regular 100-cards set, +4 HEX. This time, the whole set revolves around the 3rd Mini album, In a Million Noises, I’ll be your Harmony.
While it can be said that this comes late, in the beginning, the card-making process took so long that it was actually started before the release of Ceremony, while still working in parallel on regular updates (i.e. events).
Readers may want to know what is different from the previous set, so I will go ahead and discuss about all the important features that distinguish this set.

The concept

This set is designed to carry a concept of its own. While Awakening of Fake Idols focused on bringing the best moments from every era until Algorithm’s Blossom, while keeping a main trait with the [Fake] synergy, this time the whole set revolves around the growth of QWER as a band.
